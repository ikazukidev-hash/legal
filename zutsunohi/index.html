Privacy Policy

Effective: November 22, 2026
Last updated: November 24, 2026
App: Lights Low (a headache and migraine diary with a visit summary)

Lights Low (the "App") is provided by KAZUKI SAWASHIMA (the "Developer"). The App lets you keep a diary of your headaches, symptoms and medicines on your iPhone. Your diary stays on your device. The Developer does not receive your headaches, symptoms, medicines, notes, or the files you export. The only information that leaves your device is the usage statistics and purchase information described below.

Information stored on your device

Headaches (start and end times, how bad they were), symptoms without pain, clear days, medicine names and when you took them, preventive treatments and their start dates, and notes are stored in the App's private storage on your iPhone (SQLite). The App has no way to send this information to the Developer or to anyone else. The App does not create an account and does not use iCloud Drive or CloudKit.

Apple Health

Only if you turn on "Apple Health" in Settings, the App writes the headaches you log to the Health app as headache entries with their severity. It does not read any data from Health. This is off by default. Information written to Health is not sent to the Developer, to analytics services, or to any other third party, and is never used for advertising or marketing. If you edit or delete a headache in the App, the matching entry in Health is updated or deleted. You can review and delete Health data, and change the App's permission, in the Health app.

Evening check-in notifications

If you turn on the evening check-in, the App schedules a local notification on your iPhone at the time you choose. Answering "No headache" marks that day as a clear day in the App. Notifications are created and handled on your device and are not sent to the Developer or any third party. You can turn them off in the App or in iOS Settings at any time.

Visit summary, CSV and backup files

The visit summary PDF, CSV export and backup file are created on your device only when you ask for them. You choose where to save or send them with the iOS share sheet. Once a file leaves the App, it is handled under the terms of the place you saved or sent it to. These files never reach the Developer automatically.

Usage statistics

To find and fix what is hard to use, the App sends usage statistics. This is on by default. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > "Share usage statistics", and the App works the same either way. Nothing is sent from development builds, simulators or automated tests, and test installs (TestFlight and the purchase sandbox) are skipped when the App can detect them.

  • What is sent: the app name, the name of the action, the app version, the date and time it was sent, and a random ID created on your device. Actions are: opened the app, saved a record (sent at most once a day, with the date only and no time, whatever was recorded), opened the visit summary, shared a PDF, exported a CSV, exported or restored a backup, turned on the evening check-in, turned on Apple Health, showed the Lights Low Pro screen, tapped a plan (which plan), purchased (which plan), restored purchases, and turned statistics off. The first time the App is opened and the first time the Pro screen is shown are also counted once.
  • What is never sent: whether you had a headache, how bad it was, symptoms, medicine names, dates of your records, notes, the number of records, Health data, your name, email address, advertising identifier (IDFA), device identifiers, or location.
  • Where it is sent: a server run by the Developer (m.kurabedoki.com), which stores the date and the items above but not your IP address, and the analytics service PostHog (us.i.posthog.com). Like any internet request, PostHog receives your IP address when the App sends to it; the App asks PostHog not to look up a location from it, and the Developer does not use it to identify you.
  • The random ID is used only to count actions from the same device. It is not matched with other apps or other companies' data and is not used for tracking or advertising.

Device backups

The App does not save your diary to any cloud on its own. Depending on your iPhone settings, app data may be included in your iCloud or computer backups of the device. Deleting records or the App does not remove past device backups, files you exported, or entries written to Health. Manage those where they are stored.

Purchases

Lights Low Pro is sold through App Store in-app purchase. Apple processes payment; the Developer never receives or stores your card number. Purchases are managed with RevenueCat, which processes your purchase history, an anonymous user ID assigned by RevenueCat, and subscription expiration dates to confirm purchases, provide features, and understand purchase status.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address, your message and any attachments only to respond. If you attach records, please send only what is needed.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

What the App is

The App is a personal diary for recording your own headaches, symptoms and medicines and showing the number of days you recorded. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose any condition, predict headaches, identify causes, or tell you what or how much medicine to take. The counts and summaries only reflect what you entered. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your headaches and your medicines. If you have a sudden severe headache, a headache with fever, stiff neck, weakness, numbness, confusion or trouble speaking, or after a head injury, seek emergency care immediately.
