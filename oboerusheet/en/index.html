Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-12
Last updated: 2026-11-07
App: Oboeru Sheet (a memorization app that hides parts of photos and PDFs of your study materials and quizzes you)

Oboeru Sheet ("the App") is an app that imports photos and PDFs of textbooks, handouts, and notes that you provide, hides the parts you want to memorize to quiz you, and decides your review days. In this policy, the provider of the App is called "the Developer," and the policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

The App has no account sign-up, and you can use it without registering your name or email address. The App shows no ads. There is no feature that sends your study material images, material names, question text and answers, or review records to the Developer's servers.

The App communicates externally only for "Usage stats" in the next section (only in versions that support it) and for in-app purchases and checking purchase status (see the "Purchases" section). If you contact the Developer by email, the Developer receives your reply address and message and uses them to respond.

Usage stats

Starting with the versions that support usage stats, the App sends usage stats to help improve the App. They are on by default, and the App tells you about this once when you first open it. If you choose "Don't send" there, nothing is sent. Afterward, you can turn them off at any time with "Share usage stats" in Settings. Nothing is sent until you respond to the notice (actions you took before responding are sent together when you choose OK).

  • What is sent: the app name; the name of an action (for example: added a study material, solved questions, opened the purchase prompt); the type, count, or result of that action (for example: the number of questions solved and the number memorized); the date and time of the action; the number of days since you started using the App; the App version and build number; and a random ID generated on your device
  • What is not sent: study material images, material names, question text and answers, review records, your name, your email address
  • Random ID: created for each device and used to group actions from the same device. It is deleted when you delete the App
  • Tracking: none. The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not link this data with other companies' data
  • Where it goes: the analytics service PostHog (PostHog, Inc.) and a counting server run by the Developer (m.kurabedoki.com). The counting server receives the app name, the event name, the App version, and the random ID for each device (at launch, it also receives the number of days since you started using the App, and whether the App was newly launched or brought back to the foreground). The counting server does not store this ID as it is. It converts it into a form that cannot be reversed (a hash) and uses it only to count whether the App is still in use 1 day, 7 days, and 30 days after you started using it
  • IP address: because of how internet connections work, each destination receives the IP address the request comes from, and PostHog may store it with the records. The App has turned off PostHog's feature that estimates location from IP addresses. The counting server does not store IP addresses. The App does not use your device's location (GPS)
  • Retention: records sent to PostHog are kept according to PostHog's terms (according to PostHog, 1 year on free plans and 7 years on paid plans). The counting server deletes the record of each hashed ID 400 days after the last day it received that ID. Daily counts of actions and the number of devices that keep using the App are kept as counts that do not distinguish individual devices

If you turn them off, nothing is sent after that (when you turn them off in Settings, only one event showing that they were turned off is sent just before they stop. Communication with RevenueCat to verify purchases is separate from this setting. If you chose "Don't send" in the notice, that one event is not sent either). Turning them off does not change which features you can use.

Importing photos and PDFs

You import study materials from photos you pick on the iOS photo selection screen and from PDFs you pick on the file selection screen. The App does not ask for permission to access your entire photo library, and it reads only the photos and PDFs you select. The imported content is converted to images for studying and stored on your device; your original photos and PDFs are not changed. Importing is done only on your device, and the images are not sent outside it.

Data on your device

Study material images, material names, hidden areas, question text and answers, and review records are stored in storage on your device that is dedicated to the App. Deleting the App also deletes the materials and review records on that device. Depending on your device's settings, these are included in device backups such as iCloud. Copies of device backups are not deleted when you delete the App, so please manage them where they are stored. For details, see Apple's explanation of iCloud backups.

Backup export and restore

When you tap "Export backup" in Settings, the App puts your study material images, questions, and review records into a single file (JSON format) and exports it to a location you choose on the iOS file-saving screen. "Restore backup" reads, within your device, the file you choose and replaces your current materials and review records. The contents from before the replacement are kept on your device as a pre-restore copy. The Developer never receives or views these files. Exported files are not deleted when you delete the App, so please manage them where you saved them.

When you choose a folder under "Auto backup location" in Settings, the App writes a file with the same contents to the chosen folder (you can choose iCloud Drive too) at that moment, and each time you close the App after your materials or review records have changed. It keeps the newest 3 files and deletes older auto backup files. The location of the chosen folder is stored on your device. You can stop this with "Stop auto backup."

Review notifications

When you turn on "Notify me at review time" in Settings, the App notifies you of the number of questions to review that day, up to once a day, at the time you set (8 p.m. at first), when there are questions to review. It is off by default. The App asks for notification permission when you turn this switch on, or when you choose "Notify me" in the one-time prompt shown after you finish studying or after you close the screen where you made questions. The notification is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone; it does not use the Developer's servers or a push notification system. Notifications do not show material names or the contents of questions. You can also change notification permission in your iPhone's Settings.

Purchases

The App's PRO (monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, and a one-time purchase) is provided through Apple's App Store (StoreKit) and RevenueCat, Inc. ("RevenueCat"), a service that checks purchase status. In the App Store for Japan, PRO is offered as a one-time purchase (Lifetime) and a monthly subscription (Monthly). In the App Stores for all other countries and regions, PRO is offered as a yearly subscription (Yearly; a 7-day free trial may be included), a monthly subscription (Monthly), and a one-time purchase (Lifetime). Payment details such as credit card numbers are processed by Apple and are not received by the Developer or RevenueCat.

The RevenueCat SDK communicates with RevenueCat's servers when you open the purchase screen, when you purchase or restore purchases, and when you open the App or bring it back to the foreground on a device that has already purchased PRO (to confirm that the purchase has not been revoked). After you open the purchase screen, it may also communicate when the App comes back to the foreground, until the App is closed. On a device that has not purchased PRO, it does not communicate until you open the purchase screen. What is sent is a random anonymous ID that the SDK creates on your device, transaction information for purchases and restores (product ID, price, currency, transaction ID, receipt, and so on), device and app information (OS version, device model, App version, language settings, and so on), the iOS vendor ID (IDFV), and the current iOS tracking permission status. The App has no feature that asks you for tracking permission. Study material images, material names, question text and answers, and review records are not sent. Your name and email address are not sent either. RevenueCat's handling follows RevenueCat's privacy policy.

The service

The App hides parts of the study materials you import to quiz you, and decides and shows the next day to review based on your recorded answers. Questions you remembered return to the review list after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, or 60 days, depending on how many times in a row you remembered them. Questions you got wrong return after 10 minutes. Review days are calculated by the App's own rules and do not guarantee learning outcomes or exam results.

Handling of study materials

The study materials you import into the App are ones you provide yourself. Please use copyrighted works such as textbooks, workbooks, and handouts for your own studying. If you give an exported backup to someone else, you may need permission from the rights holder of the materials.
