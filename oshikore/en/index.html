Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-08-11
Last updated: 2026-11-07
App: Mottecho (formerly "Oshikore"; an app for recording and managing fan merchandise)

Mottecho (formerly named "Oshikore"; "the App") is provided by KAZUKI SAWASHIMA ("the Developer"). It is an app for recording and managing oshi-katsu merchandise, meaning goods connected to your favorite idols, characters, and other "oshi" (the people or characters you support). This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

The App does not send your personal information to external servers or collect it. There is no account sign-up, and the App shows no ads. The only information sent outside your device is the "usage stats" described in the next section and the in-app purchase information described under "Purchases."

The merchandise information you enter in the App (what you record, such as names, photos, prices, categories, and the names of your favorites) is stored on your device. In versions that support automatic backups to iCloud Drive, a backup copy is also saved to your own iCloud Drive, as described under "Automatic backups to iCloud Drive." No third party, including the Developer, can access this information.

Usage stats

The App sends usage stats to help improve the App. They are on by default, and you can turn them off at any time with "Send Usage Statistics" in Settings.

  • What is sent: the app name; the name of an action (for example: recorded an item, cut out a photo, opened the purchase prompt); the type, count, or result of that action; the date and time of the action; the App version and build number; and an ID generated on your device (made from the time it was created and a random number)
  • What is not sent: merchandise names, the names of your favorites, photos, amounts, notes, your name, your email address
  • Random ID: created for each device and used to group actions from the same device. It is deleted when you delete the App
  • Tracking: none. The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not link this data with other companies' data
  • Where it goes: the analytics service PostHog (PostHog, Inc.) and a counting server run by the Developer (m.kurabedoki.com). The counting server receives only the app name, the event name, and the App version, and keeps only daily counts. Events are sent to the counting server when the App is opened for the first time on a device and when the PRO screen is shown. The random ID for each device is not sent to the counting server
  • IP address: because of how internet connections work, each destination receives the IP address the request comes from, and PostHog may store it with the records. The App has turned off PostHog's feature that estimates location from IP addresses. The counting server does not store IP addresses. The App does not use your device's location (GPS)
  • Retention: records sent to PostHog are kept according to PostHog's terms (according to PostHog, 1 year on free plans and 7 years on paid plans). The counting server keeps no records for individual devices

The App tells you about sending usage stats once, inside the App. If you choose "Don't Send," or turn off "Send Usage Statistics" in Settings, nothing is sent after that. Communication with RevenueCat to verify purchases is separate from this setting. Turning it off does not change which features you can use. In earlier versions that do not have "Send Usage Statistics," data is sent only if you allowed it on the confirmation screen or under "Usage records" in Settings.

Data on your device

Data recorded in the App is stored in the App's private storage on your device. Deleting the App deletes this data from your device (backup copies saved to iCloud Drive are not deleted). To back up or move your data, use the automatic iCloud Drive backup below or the file export described under "Sharing and export." The "Didn't Buy" records and the matching data the App creates from photos for "Check by photo" are also stored on your device, but they are not included in automatic backups or in exported backups.

Automatic backups to iCloud Drive

In versions that support automatic backups to iCloud Drive, when "Auto Backup" under "Backups" in Settings is on (it is on by default), and at least 24 hours have passed since the last backup when you open the App, the App saves your records (merchandise names, names of your favorites, categories, boxes, quantities, prices, dates, notes, and so on) and photos in the "Mottecho" folder of the iCloud Drive linked to your Apple Account (the "推しコレ" (Oshikore) folder in earlier versions). Each photo is saved only once, based on its contents, and photos already in the previous backup are not rewritten. The App keeps the last 7 days of backups and deletes older backups and any photos that no backup uses anymore. Apple's iCloud stores and syncs the files; the Developer does not receive or view them. If iCloud Drive is unavailable or your iCloud storage does not have enough space, the backup is saved only in the App's private storage on your device. If a single backup would exceed 500 MB, it is not saved, and "Backups" in Settings shows a note about this.

You can turn automatic backups off under "Backups" in Settings at any time. You can delete saved backups in the Files app under iCloud Drive > Mottecho (the "推しコレ" (Oshikore) folder in earlier versions). Backups in iCloud Drive remain even if you delete the App. On a new device or after reinstalling, if the App has no records, it asks once whether to restore from a backup it finds. If one is found after you have started using the App, it only shows a note on the Merch screen and does not replace your records. Your records from before a restore are kept in the App's private storage on your device.

CSV import

Under "Import" in Settings, you can add the contents of a CSV file that you pick in the Files app as records. The file is read only on your device, and its contents are never sent outside your device.

Photos and camera

You choose merchandise photos with your device's photo picker, so the App does not ask for permission to access your entire photo library. The App does not read anything in your library other than the photos you select, and does not send photos outside your device. Selected photos are copied to the App's private storage on your device and are handled like the on-device data described above.

The App asks for camera permission when you take a photo of merchandise to record it, and when you compare against merchandise you already own with "Check by photo" (including "Snap to Check," which appears when you long-press the App's icon on the Home Screen). The comparison is done only on your device, and the photos used for it are not sent outside your device.

When you choose "Save Image" in the share sheet for a merchandise image or a summary image, the App adds only that image to your photo library, with your permission through iOS.

Notifications

The App uses local notifications on your device for the reminder on the day before the expected release date of an item on your wishlist, and for the Monday and 1st-of-the-month notices that ask people who have wishlist items to check their schedule. They are off at first; after you save a wishlist item with an expected release date, the App asks for notification permission, and they turn on if you allow it. You can turn them off at any time with "Day-Before Release Reminder" in Settings. Notification content is not sent outside your device. You can change notification permission in your device's Settings at any time.

Sharing and export

Separately from the automatic backups described above, the App has a "Backup File" feature that exports your data to a file (including restoring from that file) and a sharing feature that exports an image summarizing your records. These work only when you explicitly start them on screen, and you choose where to send them (mail, messaging apps, saving to Files, and so on) through your device's share sheet. The Developer never receives or views these files.

Purchases

Purchases in the App (PRO: monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, and a one-time purchase) are provided through App Store in-app purchase. In the App Store for Japan, PRO is offered as a one-time purchase (Lifetime) and a monthly subscription (Monthly). In the App Stores for all other countries and regions, PRO is offered as a yearly subscription (Yearly; a 7-day free trial may be included), a monthly subscription (Monthly), and a one-time purchase (Lifetime). Purchases are managed with RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a payment platform, which processes your purchase history, a user ID, and similar information. The RevenueCat SDK communicates from the time the App starts, so even if you have not purchased PRO, it sends an automatically generated app user ID, the device identifier IDFV when available, the device type, OS, app, and SDK versions, region and currency settings, and similar information. It also sends the current iOS tracking permission status, but the App has no feature that asks for that permission. Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Apple and RevenueCat; the Developer does not obtain or store them. The App uses only your purchase status (whether you have purchased) to decide which features are available.
