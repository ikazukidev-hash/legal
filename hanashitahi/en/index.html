Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-07
Last updated: 2026-11-07
App: Kazoe Talk (an app that tallies your LINE chat history on your device)

Kazoe Talk ("the App") is provided by KAZUKI SAWASHIMA ("the Developer"). It reads a text file exported with LINE's "Send chat history" feature and counts things such as the number of days you talked, the number of messages, and how quickly you reply. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

The App does not send the text of messages, the other person's name, or the dates and times contained in the imported chat history to the Developer's servers or to any third party's servers. No AI or external server is used to analyze the chat history; all tallying, such as the number of days you talked and how quickly you reply, is done only by processing on your device. The App communicates externally only for the two purposes described below, "Usage stats" and "Checking purchase information" (see "Purchases"), and neither includes the content of your chats. A share card hands an image that includes the other person's display name and the period to the iOS share sheet only when you perform a sharing action (you choose where it goes). There is no account sign-up, and the App shows no ads.

Usage stats

The App sends usage stats to help improve the App. They are on by default, and the App tells you about this once when you open it. Nothing is sent until you choose "OK" in the notice. You can turn them off at any time with "Don't send" in the notice or with "Share usage stats" in Settings.

  • What is sent: the app name; the name of an action (for example: imported a chat, opened a summary, opened the purchase prompt); the type, count, or result of that action; the date and time of the action; the App version and build number; and a random ID generated on your device
  • What is not sent: the text of chats, the other person's name, the dates and times of messages, your name, your email address
  • Random ID: created for each device and used to group actions from the same device. It is deleted when you delete the App
  • Tracking: none. The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for usage stats and does not link this data with other companies' data
  • Where it goes: the analytics service PostHog (PostHog, Inc.) and a counting server run by the Developer (m.kurabedoki.com). The counting server receives only the app name, the event name, and the App version; the random ID is not sent to it
  • IP address: because of how internet connections work, each destination receives the IP address the request comes from, and PostHog may store it with the records. The App has turned off PostHog's feature that estimates location from IP addresses. The counting server does not store IP addresses. The App does not use your device's location (GPS)
  • Retention: records sent to PostHog are kept according to PostHog's terms (according to PostHog, 1 year on free plans and 7 years on paid plans). The counting server keeps only daily counts

If you turn them off, nothing is sent after that (only when you turn them off in Settings, one event showing that they were turned off is sent just before they stop). Communication with RevenueCat to verify purchases is separate from this setting. Turning them off does not change which features you can use. Counts are sent only as rough orders of magnitude (for example, a message count of "1,000 to 10,000"). In earlier versions that do not have "Share usage stats," data is sent only if you allowed it on the confirmation screen or under "Usage records" in Settings.

Data on your device

The chat history files you import are saved as copies in the App's Documents folder, and the tallied results are stored in a database on your device (SQLite). You can delete this data chat by chat, or all at once with the "Delete everything" feature. Deleting the App also deletes this data from your device. Depending on your device's iCloud or computer backup settings, this data may be included in those backups (whether it is included follows the settings on your device). No third party, including the Developer, can access this data.

Sharing and export

The App has a sharing feature that exports an image summarizing the tallied results. It works only when you explicitly start it on screen, and you choose where to send it (messaging apps, social media, saving to Files, and so on) through your device's share sheet. The Developer never receives or views this image.

Notifications

A reminder to re-import (once every 7 days after your last import) is scheduled and shown on your device only if you allow notifications. The reminder does not show the other person's name or the contents of the chat. The notification content and its scheduling stay on your device and are not sent to the Developer's servers or to any third party. You can turn the reminder off with "Re-import reminder" in Settings, and you can also change notification permission in your device's Settings.

Purchases

Purchases in the App (PRO: monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, and a one-time purchase) are provided through App Store in-app purchase (using RevenueCat as the payment platform). In the App Store for Japan, PRO is offered as a one-time purchase (Lifetime) and a monthly subscription (Monthly). In the App Stores for all other countries and regions, PRO is offered as a yearly subscription (Yearly; a 7-day free trial may be included), a monthly subscription (Monthly), and a one-time purchase (Lifetime). Payment details such as card numbers are processed by Apple, and neither the Developer nor RevenueCat obtains or stores them. The App uses only your purchase status (whether you have purchased and whether you are within the paid period) to decide which features are available.

The App communicates with RevenueCat from the time it starts, so information is sent even if you have not purchased PRO. The information sent to RevenueCat includes an automatically generated app user ID, the device identifier IDFV when available, the device type, OS, app, and SDK versions, and region and currency settings. For purchases and restores, the information handled includes the product identifier, the purchase date and time, transaction and receipt information, and purchase status. The current iOS tracking permission status is also sent, but the App has no feature that asks for that permission. In addition, the App passes to RevenueCat attribution information issued by Apple that shows whether you downloaded the App from an Apple Ads ad and, if so, the campaign, ad group, keyword, and similar details. The information described above is used to confirm purchases, provide features, and understand purchase status and the effectiveness of ads. The content of your chats and the other person's name are not sent.

The service

The App imports a text file that you exported with LINE's "Send chat history" feature, and tallies and displays statistical figures on your device, such as the number of days you talked, the number of messages, each person's share, times of day, reply speed, and the "Pair Type." The figures shown are based on what is written in the imported file and the App's analysis processing, and the Developer does not guarantee their accuracy. The results, including the "Pair Type," are explanations of numbers. They do not judge whether two people are compatible, and they are not advice or diagnosis about romance or relationships.

The results include fragments of 2 to 12 characters cut out from the message text, shown as frequently used phrases. These are also stored only on your device and are not sent outside it.

The other person's messages

The chat history the App imports includes what people other than you said. Please use the App only to import and view chats you took part in, for your own use. If you publish or share the results or screens, do so with the other person's consent, and take care not to harm their interests, such as their reputation and privacy.
