Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-07
Last updated: 2026-10-31
App: Dekitabi (habit tracker)

Dekitabi ("the App") is provided by KAZUKI SAWASHIMA ("the Developer"). It lets you record the days you kept a habit you want to build or quit. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name or email address. There is no account sign-up and no advertising. The only information sent outside your device is the "usage stats" described below (which you can turn off in Settings at any time) and the information needed to process in-app purchases.

Usage stats are on by default. After you set up your first habit, the App shows a one-time notice that it sends usage stats. If you choose "Don’t send" there, or turn off "Share usage stats" in Settings, nothing is sent. Nothing is sent until you respond to the notice, and choosing "Don’t send" discards anything collected up to that point. Only the following information is sent, to help improve the App:

  • The name of an action (for example: saved a check, opened Review, closed the PRO screen)
  • The type, count, or result of that action (for example: whether a note was added, the type of habit created, whether a reminder time was set, which screen opened the PRO screen, how many items a backup contained, whether the App was opened from a notification and which kind, how many days were filled in at once, whether an amount or price was entered for a quit habit)
  • A random anonymous ID generated on your device (used only to group actions from the same device; it cannot identify you)
  • The date and time of the action, and the App version

This information is sent to an analytics service (PostHog). Location is not collected (the App explicitly disables estimating location from your IP address). The content you record, such as habit names, the days you checked, and notes, is never sent. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

Under the same setting (when "Share usage stats" is on), the App also counts two events: the first time the App is opened on a device (counted once, when you agree) and each time the Dekitabi PRO screen is shown. Nothing is sent if you have not agreed.

  • What is sent: the app name, the event name, and the App version only
  • What is not sent: device identifiers, the anonymous ID, the date and time, habit names, notes
  • Where it goes: a counting server run by the Developer (m.kurabedoki.com). IP addresses are not stored; only daily counts are kept
  • Purpose: to understand how often the PRO screen is shown and improve the App. It is not used to identify you, combined with other information, or used for advertising

What you enter in the App (habit names, types, weekdays, reminder times, checked days, notes) is stored on your device. In versions that support automatic backups to iCloud Drive, a backup copy is also saved to your own iCloud Drive as described below. The Developer does not receive this content.

Data on your device

Data recorded in the App is stored in the App's private storage on your device. Deleting the App deletes this data from your device (backup copies saved to iCloud Drive are not deleted). To back up or move your data, use the automatic iCloud Drive backup below or the file export described under "Sharing and export."

Automatic backups to iCloud Drive

When "Back up automatically" under Auto backup in Settings is on (it is on by default), and at least 24 hours have passed since the last backup when you open the App, the App saves your records (habit names, types, weekdays, reminder times, checked days, notes) as a single JSON file in the "Dekitabi" folder of the iCloud Drive linked to your Apple Account. The App keeps the last 7 days of backups and deletes older ones. Apple's iCloud stores and syncs the file; the Developer never receives or views it. If iCloud Drive is unavailable or your iCloud storage is full, the backup is saved only in the App's private storage on your device.

You can turn automatic backups off under Auto backup in Settings at any time. You can delete saved backups in the Files app under iCloud Drive > Dekitabi. Backups in iCloud Drive remain even if you delete the App. On a new device or after reinstalling, if the App has no records, it asks once whether to restore from a backup it finds. If one is found after you have started using the App, it only shows a note on the Today screen and does not replace your records.

Sharing and export

Separately from automatic backups, the App can export your data to a file (and restore from that file), export CSV for spreadsheets, and export an image summarizing your records. These work only when you explicitly start them on screen, and you choose where to send them (mail, messages, Files, and so on) through your device's share sheet. The Developer never receives or views these files.

Purchases

Purchases in the App (PRO: monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, and a one-time purchase) are provided through App Store in-app purchase. Purchases are managed with RevenueCat, which processes your purchase history, a user ID, and subscription expiration dates. Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Apple and RevenueCat; the Developer does not obtain or store them. The App uses only your purchase status (whether you have purchased and whether a subscription is active) to decide which features are available.

The service

The App counts and displays the checks you make as a number of days. The numbers shown are based on your input, and the Developer does not guarantee their accuracy. The App does not provide medical or health advice, and it is not a treatment for quitting drinking or smoking. Reminders (notifications) are scheduled on your device only if you allow them (the daily reminder can be turned on or off in Settings and is off by default), and may not arrive depending on your device's settings or state.

PRO (auto-renewing subscriptions and one-time purchase)

  • Names: Dekitabi PRO (Monthly) / Dekitabi PRO (Yearly) / Dekitabi PRO (Lifetime)
  • Includes: a 4th habit and beyond, calendar history older than 90 days and a year overview, rest days that keep your streak going (up to 2 a month), and app color themes
  • Price and length: shown on the purchase screen in the App (Monthly is 1 month, Yearly is 1 year; Lifetime is a single payment)
  • Monthly and Yearly renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. After purchase, you can manage or cancel at any time in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your device
  • Refunds for periods already paid follow Apple's policies
