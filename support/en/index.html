Support

FAQ

My PRO purchase doesn't show up on a new device
Tap "Restore Purchases" on the PRO screen or in Settings. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I move my records to a new phone?
Each app has a backup in Settings. Some apps also save backup copies to your own iCloud Drive automatically. Export a backup file before you change phones, then restore it on the new phone. What each backup includes is described in each app's privacy policy below.
Is my data sent anywhere?
Your records are stored on your device (and in your own iCloud Drive if the app makes backup copies there). If usage stats are on, the app sends action names, counts, dates, the app version, and a random ID created on your device. You can turn this off in Settings. Tanoshimi Diet sends meal photos and dish names to a server only to make calorie estimates. Details are in each app's privacy policy.
How do I cancel a subscription?
On your iPhone, go to Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Your records stay even after you cancel.
What happens if I delete an app?
Records stored on the device are deleted too. Backup copies you saved to iCloud Drive or exported as files remain. Export a backup before deleting.
