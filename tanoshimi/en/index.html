Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-06
Last updated: 2026-11-07
App: Tanoshimi Kakeibo (a household budget app that divides your money into envelopes)

Tanoshimi Kakeibo ("the App") is provided by KAZUKI SAWASHIMA ("the Developer"). It lets you record your spending by money envelope (budget). This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

The App does not send your personal information to external servers or collect it. There is no account sign-up, and the App shows no ads. The only information sent outside your device is the "usage stats" described in the next section and the in-app purchase information described under "Purchases."

Everything you enter in the App (envelope names, budgets, amounts, dates, notes, and events; the budgets, dates, and plans of events; monthly recurring items; and the date you became a fan of your favorite) is stored only on your device. No third party, including the Developer, can access this information.

Usage stats

The App sends usage stats to help improve the App. They are on by default, and you can turn them off at any time with "Send Usage Stats" in Settings.

  • What is sent: the app name; the name of an action (for example: recorded an expense, added an envelope, opened the purchase prompt); the App version; the day of the action (the date on your device's calendar); and a random ID generated on your device. To PostHog, the App also sends the date and time of the action, where the purchase screen was opened from, which plan was purchased, and the answer you chose as the reason for closing the purchase screen. It also sends one event each to the counting server the first time the App is opened on this device and the first time the PRO screen is shown
  • What is not sent: envelope names, budgets, amounts, notes, events, your name, your email address
  • Random ID: created for each device and used to group actions from the same device. It is deleted when you delete the App
  • Tracking: none. The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not link this data with other companies' data
  • Where it goes: the analytics service PostHog (PostHog, Inc., USA) and a counting server run by the Developer (m.kurabedoki.com). The counting server does not store the random ID as it is. It converts it into a form that cannot be reversed (a hash) and uses it only to count whether the App is still in use 1 day, 7 days, and 30 days after you started using it
  • IP address: because of how internet connections work, each destination receives the IP address the request comes from, and PostHog may store it with the records. The App has turned off PostHog's feature that estimates location from IP addresses. The counting server does not store IP addresses. The App does not use your device's location (GPS)
  • Retention: records sent to PostHog are kept according to PostHog's terms (according to PostHog, 1 year on free plans and 7 years on paid plans). The counting server stores only the converted value of the random ID, not the ID itself, to count continued use, and deletes each value 400 days after the last day it received that value

The App tells you about sending usage stats once, inside the App. If you choose "Don't Send," or turn off "Send Usage Stats" in Settings, nothing is sent after that (only when you turn it off in Settings, one event showing that it was turned off is sent just before it stops). Communication with RevenueCat to verify purchases is separate from this setting. Turning it off does not change which features you can use. In earlier versions that do not have "Send Usage Stats," data is sent to the analytics service PostHog (PostHog, Inc.) and the counting server only if you allowed it on the confirmation screen or under "Usage records" in Settings.

Data on your device

Data recorded in the App is stored in the App's private storage on your device. Deleting the App deletes this data from your device. If you need to back up or move your data, export a file yourself, at your own discretion, using the features described under "Sharing and export" below.

To show information on the Home Screen widget, the App also saves envelope names and this month's remaining and spent amounts in storage on your device that is shared with the widget. When "Lock with Face ID" is on, the widget hides the amounts.

Face ID lock

When you turn on "Lock with Face ID" in Settings, your device verifies that it is you, with biometric authentication such as Face ID or with your device passcode, when you open the App and after you have been in other apps for 30 seconds or more. The verification is done by your device's OS, and the App does not receive any facial or fingerprint information. It is off by default.

Notifications

The App uses local notifications on your device for the following. Notification content is not sent outside your device. You can change notification permission in your device's Settings at any time.

  • A reminder on days when you have not yet recorded anything (off by default; you choose the time under "Reminder on Days Without Entries" in Settings). Reminders the day before a trip or event, and start-of-week and start-of-month reminders, also follow this setting
  • A notice on the morning after a month closes, to look back on the previous month (off by default; "Month Closing Reminder" in Settings)
  • If you started the yearly plan (Yearly) with a free trial, a reminder before the free trial ends

Sharing and export

The App has a "Backup" feature that exports your data to a file (including restoring from that file), CSV export for spreadsheets, and a sharing feature that exports an image summarizing your records. These work only when you explicitly start them on screen, and you choose where to send them (mail, messaging apps, saving to Files, and so on) through your device's share sheet. The Developer never receives or views these files.

When you save a share card image to Photos, the App adds it to your photo library with your permission through iOS. The App does not read the photos in your library.

Purchases

Purchases in the App (PRO: monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, and a one-time purchase) are provided through App Store in-app purchase. In the App Store for Japan, PRO is offered as a one-time purchase (Lifetime) and a monthly subscription (Monthly). In the App Stores for all other countries and regions, PRO is offered as a yearly subscription (Yearly; a 7-day free trial may be included), a monthly subscription (Monthly), and a one-time purchase (Lifetime). Purchases are managed with RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a payment platform, which processes your purchase history, a user ID, subscription expiration dates, and similar information. The RevenueCat SDK communicates from the time the App starts, so even if you have not purchased PRO, it sends an automatically generated app user ID, the device identifier IDFV when available, the device type, OS, app, and SDK versions, region and currency settings, and similar information. It also sends the current iOS tracking permission status, but the App has no feature that asks for that permission. In addition, the App passes to RevenueCat attribution information issued by Apple that shows whether you downloaded the App from an Apple Ads ad and, if so, the campaign, ad group, keyword, and similar details. The information described above is used to confirm purchases, provide features, and understand purchase status and the effectiveness of ads. Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Apple and RevenueCat; the Developer does not obtain or store them. The App uses only your purchase status (whether you have purchased and whether you are within the paid period) to decide which features are available.
