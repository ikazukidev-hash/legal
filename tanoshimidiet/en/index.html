Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-06
Last updated: 2026-11-07
App: Tanoshimi Diet (meal logging with photos and a weight graph)

Tanoshimi Diet ("the App") is provided by KAZUKI SAWASHIMA ("the Developer"). It lets you record your meals and weight. Your records are stored on your device. For AI estimates, usage stats, purchases, and sharing, the App sends the information described below outside your device.

Photo library and camera

You choose photos with your device's photo picker, so the App does not ask for permission to access your entire photo library. When you take a photo of your meal in the App, it asks for camera permission. Only when you choose "Save Image" on the sharing screen does the App add the card it created to your photo library, with your permission through iOS.

When estimating from a meal photo or a dish name

For an estimate from a photo, the App sends a reduced-size version of the photo you took or selected. For an estimate from a dish name, it sends the dish name and amount you entered. They are sent to a server operated by the Developer (Cloudflare Workers). The server uses Cloudflare Workers AI, or Anthropic's API if the Developer has enabled it, and returns an estimate of the dish name, calories, and nutrients. If you only save a photo or enter a record by hand, the content is not sent to an analysis service unless you run an AI estimate (except for photos waiting for an estimate, described in the next paragraph).

A photo you take or select after using up that day's allowance of estimates is kept on your device as a photo waiting for an estimate. When you open the App or bring it back to the foreground after 8 p.m. Japan time, and you still have estimates left for that day, the waiting photos are automatically sent for estimation, oldest first. A record for which you entered the dish name first does not become a waiting photo.

To count how many estimates you use, each estimate request includes a random ID that the App generates and stores on your device. To check whether you have PRO, the request also includes your user ID in the purchase platform (RevenueCat). The random ID is not your name, but it can be used to recognize continued use of the same app. The server also receives the source IP address when the request is made. If a photo or dish name contains personal information, that information is sent as well.

The Developer's server program does not write the photos, dish names, or estimate results it receives to a storage database. To manage usage counts, it temporarily stores values made by combining IDs, IP addresses, and similar data with the date and converting them, the processing result, and an expiration time, and it also stores the result of the purchase entitlement check for a short time. Processing logs used to investigate failures are kept according to the server platform's settings.

Handling by these analysis services is also subject to each provider's terms. Cloudflare states that it does not use inputs and outputs passed to Workers AI for model training or service improvement without explicit consent. By default, Anthropic's API deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days, but there are exceptions for contracts, responses to violations of its terms, legal requirements, and so on. For details, see Cloudflare's data usage policy and Anthropic's retention periods.

Usage stats

The App sends usage stats to help improve the App. They are on by default, and you can turn them off at any time with "Send usage data" in Settings.

  • What is sent: the app name; the name of an action (for example: recorded a meal, estimated from a photo, recorded weight, opened the purchase prompt); the App version; and a random ID generated on your device. To PostHog, the App also sends the date and time of the action, where the purchase screen was opened from, which plan was purchased, and the answer you chose as the reason for closing the purchase screen. It also sends one event each to the counting server the first time the App is opened on this device and the first time the PRO screen is shown
  • What is not sent: photos, dish names, weight and goal values, notes, Health values, your name, your email address
  • Random ID: created for each device and used to group actions from the same device. It is deleted when you delete the App
  • Tracking: none. The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not link this data with other companies' data
  • Where it goes: the analytics service PostHog (PostHog, Inc., USA) and a counting server run by the Developer (m.kurabedoki.com). The counting server does not store the random ID as it is. It converts it into a form that cannot be reversed (a hash) and uses it only to count whether the App is still in use 1 day, 7 days, and 30 days after you started using it
  • IP address: because of how internet connections work, each destination receives the IP address the request comes from, and PostHog may store it with the records. The App has turned off PostHog's feature that estimates location from IP addresses. The counting server does not store IP addresses. The App does not use your device's location (GPS)
  • Retention: records sent to PostHog are kept according to PostHog's terms (according to PostHog, 1 year on free plans and 7 years on paid plans). The counting server stores only the converted value of the random ID, not the ID itself, to count continued use, and deletes each value 400 days after the last day it received that value

The App tells you about sending usage stats once, inside the App. If you choose "Don't Send," or turn off "Send usage data" in Settings, nothing is sent after that. Communication with RevenueCat to verify purchases is separate from this setting. Turning it off does not change which features you can use. The random ID for usage stats is created separately from the ID used for AI estimates. In earlier versions that do not have "Send usage data," data is sent to the analytics service PostHog (PostHog, Inc.) and the counting server only if you allowed it on the confirmation screen or under "Usage records" in Settings.

Records on your device and backups

Your goals, weight, meal records, food list, and reduced-size photos (including photos waiting for an estimate) are stored in the App's private storage on your device. The original photos in the Photos app are managed separately from the App's records.

Depending on your device's settings, the App's data is included in backups to iCloud or to a computer. Deleting records from the App, or deleting the App, does not also delete past OS backups, exported files, or your original photos. Please manage each of them where it is stored. For iCloud, see Apple's backup explanation.

Apple Health integration

The App exchanges data with Apple Health (the Health app on iPhone) only if you turn on "Sync with Apple Health" on the Settings screen. It is off by default.

  • Information read: weight recorded in Health. Of the entries from the most recent 90 days and those added after that, the entries for days on which the App has no weight record are imported as records on your device
  • Information written: the weight you enter in the App, and the estimated calories for each meal (dietary energy). The dish name is attached to meal entries. If you edit a record in the App, the entry is rewritten, and if you delete a record, the matching entry is deleted
  • Information read from Health and information written to Health are not sent to the Developer's server, analytics services, or any other third party. It is not used for advertising or marketing either. Usage stats do not include the connection operations or weight and calorie values. The counts of exports and imports also include weight records read from Health
  • If you turn the connection off, reading and writing stop from then on. Weight already imported stays in the App, and information already written stays in Health. You can review and delete information in Health, and change the App's permissions, in the Health app

Notifications

The App uses local notifications on your device for the following. Notification content is not sent outside your device. You can change notification permission in your device's Settings at any time.

  • A recording reminder (off by default; you choose the time under "Meal reminder" in Settings)
  • A notice at 8 p.m. when you have photos waiting for an estimate (only if notifications are allowed)
  • If you started the yearly plan (Yearly) with a free trial, a reminder before the free trial ends

Sharing and export

Backup files, CSV files for spreadsheets, and images summarizing your records are created when you run an export. You can choose where to save or send them in the OS share sheet. How a file is handled after it reaches the place you shared it to follows the terms of that destination or service. Except when you send them to the Developer yourself to ask a question, these files are never delivered to the Developer automatically.

Purchases

PRO uses App Store in-app purchase. In the App Store for Japan, PRO is offered as a one-time purchase (Lifetime) and a monthly auto-renewing subscription (Monthly). In the App Stores for all other countries and regions, PRO is offered as a yearly auto-renewing subscription (Yearly; a 7-day free trial may be included), a monthly auto-renewing subscription (Monthly), and a one-time purchase (Lifetime). Apple processes payments, and the Developer does not obtain or store credit card numbers. Purchases are managed with RevenueCat, which processes your purchase history, a user ID, subscription expiration dates, and similar information. The RevenueCat SDK communicates from the time the App starts, so even if you have not purchased PRO, it sends an automatically generated app user ID, the device identifier IDFV when available, the device type, OS, app, and SDK versions, region and currency settings, and similar information. It also sends the current iOS tracking permission status, but the App has no feature that asks for that permission. In addition, the App passes to RevenueCat attribution information issued by Apple that shows whether you downloaded the App from an Apple Ads ad and, if so, the campaign, ad group, keyword, and similar details. The information described above is used to confirm purchases, provide features, and understand purchase status and the effectiveness of ads. To check your AI estimate limit, the Developer's server may also query RevenueCat for your subscription status.

Information received when you contact the Developer

If you contact the Developer by email, the Developer uses your reply email address, the message text, and any attached information to respond to your inquiry. If you attach records or photos, please send only what is needed to handle your inquiry.
