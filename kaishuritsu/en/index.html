Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-07
Last updated: 2026-11-07
App: Kaishuritsu (a profit and loss tracker for horse racing betting tickets)

Kaishuritsu ("the App") is provided by KAZUKI SAWASHIMA ("the Developer"). It lets you record the purchase amounts and payouts of the horse racing betting tickets you bought yourself, and it calculates your profit and loss and your return rate. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

The App has no account sign-up, and you can use it without registering your name or email address. The App shows no ads and does not obtain the advertising identifier. If you separately contact the Developer by email, the Developer receives your reply address and message and uses them to respond.

Besides the "usage stats" described in the next section, the App communicates externally only to get in-app purchase prices, process purchases, and check purchase status (Apple's StoreKit and the purchase processing service RevenueCat).

Your betting ticket records and monthly budget are stored first in the App's private storage on your device. There is no feature that syncs your betting ticket records to the Developer's servers. Usage stats, communication for purchases, and exports and sharing that you do yourself are each handled as described below.

Usage stats

The App sends usage stats to help improve the App. They are on by default, and the App tells you about this once when you open it. Usage stats are sent even before you respond to the notice. You can turn them off at any time with "Don't send" in the notice or with "Share usage stats" in Settings.

  • What is sent: the app name; the name of an action (for example: saved a ticket, opened Analysis, opened the purchase prompt); the type, count, or result of that action; the date and time of the action; the number of days since you started using the App; the App version and build number; and a random ID generated on your device
  • What is not sent: purchase amounts, payout amounts, bet selections, notes on tickets, racecourses, your monthly budget, your name, your email address
  • Random ID: created for each device and used to group actions from the same device. It is deleted when you delete the App
  • Tracking: none. The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not link this data with other companies' data
  • Where it goes: the analytics service PostHog (PostHog, Inc.) and a counting server run by the Developer (m.kurabedoki.com). The counting server receives only the app name, the event name, and the App version; the random ID is not sent to it
  • IP address: because of how internet connections work, each destination receives the IP address the request comes from, and PostHog may store it with the records. The App has turned off PostHog's feature that estimates location from IP addresses. The counting server does not store IP addresses. The App does not use your device's location (GPS)
  • Retention: records sent to PostHog are kept according to PostHog's terms (according to PostHog, 1 year on free plans and 7 years on paid plans). The counting server keeps only daily counts

If you turn them off, nothing is sent after that (only when you turn them off in Settings, one event showing that they were turned off is sent just before they stop). Communication with RevenueCat to verify purchases is separate from this setting. Turning them off does not change which features you can use. In earlier versions that do not have "Share usage stats," data is sent only if you agreed on the confirmation screen or with the switch in Settings.

Data on your device

When you delete the App, the records in the App's private storage on that device are deleted too. If you want to prepare for changing devices or similar situations, export a file with "Backup" before deleting the App. Also, depending on your device's settings, your records are included in device backups such as iCloud. Exported files and copies of device backups are not deleted when you delete the App, so please manage them where they are stored. For details, see Apple's explanation of iCloud backups.

For the Home Screen widget, the App also saves totals such as this month's profit and loss and return rate in a storage area on your device that only the App and the widget can read. If you place the widget, this month's profit and loss and return rate are shown on your Home Screen.

Export features

The App has a "Backup" feature that exports your data to a file (including importing from that exported file), CSV export for spreadsheets, and sharing of an image showing this month's profit and loss, return rate, and so on (a profit and loss card). All of these work only when you explicitly start them on screen, and you choose where to send them (mail, messaging apps, saving to Files, and so on) through your device's share sheet. The Developer never receives or views these files or images. When you import a CSV exported from another app, the App reads, within your device, the file you chose.

Notifications

"Waiting for results reminder" and "This week's return rate on Sunday night," under "Notifications" in Settings, are scheduled and shown on your device only if you allow notifications. Notifications do not show amounts or the contents of your tickets. The notification content and its scheduling stay on your device and are not sent to the Developer's servers or to any third party. You can turn each notification off with its own switch, and you can also change notification permission in your device's Settings.

Purchases

Purchases in the App (PRO: monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, and a one-time purchase) are provided through App Store in-app purchase (Apple's StoreKit) and the purchase processing service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). In the App Store for Japan, PRO is offered as a one-time purchase (Lifetime) and a monthly subscription (Monthly). In the App Stores for all other countries and regions, PRO is offered as a yearly subscription (Yearly; a 7-day free trial may be included), a monthly subscription (Monthly), and a one-time purchase (Lifetime). Payment details such as card numbers are processed by Apple, and neither the Developer nor RevenueCat obtains or stores them. The App uses only your purchase status (whether you have purchased) to decide which features are available.

The App communicates with RevenueCat from the time it starts, so information is sent even if you have not purchased PRO. The information sent to RevenueCat includes an automatically generated app user ID, the device identifier IDFV when available, the device type, OS, app, and SDK versions, and region and currency settings. For purchases and restores, the information handled includes the product identifier, the purchase date and time, transaction and receipt information, and purchase status. The current iOS tracking permission status is also sent, but the App has no feature that asks for that permission. Your betting ticket records, name, and email address are not sent. RevenueCat's handling follows its privacy policy (revenuecat.com/privacy).

The service

The App totals the purchase and payout amounts for the betting tickets that you enter yourself, and shows your profit and loss and your return rate. The numbers shown are based on your input, and the Developer does not guarantee their accuracy. The result of the ticket-count calculation is the general number of combinations; it does not guarantee whether an actual bet can be placed or what the payout will be.

The App does not in any way broker the purchase of betting tickets or the placing of bets, provide predictions, hold funds, or receive payouts. You are to purchase betting tickets yourself, in accordance with law and by the methods set by the organizer of the publicly operated race. Persons under 20 years of age cannot purchase betting tickets.
